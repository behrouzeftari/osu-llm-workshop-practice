Linux File Permissions

A three-lesson introduction for students in their first Unix course

Lesson 1: Understanding Permissions Lesson 2: Changing Permissions Lesson 3: Special Permissions & Practice

What you'll learn

  • Lesson 1: Read the ls -l output, understand rwx for owner/group/others, and decode permission strings like rwxr-xr--.
  • Lesson 2: Use chmod (symbolic and numeric), chown, and chgrp to change who can do what.
  • Lesson 3: Setuid, setgid, sticky bit — when they matter and how to spot them. Plus a hands-on scenario to tie it together.

How the check questions work

Each lesson has short "Check your understanding" boxes. Type an answer in your own words and press Check my answer. The workshop model will tell you what's right and ask one guiding question — it never gives the answer away.

On the live site (while this Codespace runs), answers are checked in real time. On the published GitHub Pages copy, the questions appear with a note that checking works on the live site.
